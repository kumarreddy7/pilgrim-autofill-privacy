Pilgrim Autofill: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Pilgrim Autofill is a browser extension that saves pilgrim details you enter and lets you copy them, one at a time, for pasting into the TTD online booking form. This policy explains what the extension does with those details.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your own computer, encrypted with your passphrase. The extension makes no network requests. The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

What you enter

  • For each pilgrim: name, age, gender and Aadhaar number.
  • Contact details: email address, city, state, country, PIN code and gotram.
  • Names you give to your lists.
  • A passphrase, which is used to encrypt the items above. The passphrase itself is never stored.

Where it is kept

  • Your lists are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your passphrase, and saved in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
  • While Chrome is open and the extension is unlocked, the key is held in Chrome's session storage, which is kept in memory and cleared when Chrome closes or when you click Lock.
  • Nothing is synced to a Google account, and nothing is stored on any server.

How it is used

  • When you click the Copy button beside a detail, that one detail is placed on your computer's clipboard so you can paste it. It stays on the clipboard until you copy something else. Once you paste it into a website, it is covered by that website's own privacy policy.
  • The extension has no access to any website. It does not read your browsing history or the content of any page, and it does not fill in or submit forms.

What is shared

Nothing. The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send data to the developer or to any third party. Your data is not sold or transferred to anyone.

Export files

If you use Export, the chosen list is saved to a file on your computer. That file is not encrypted and contains Aadhaar numbers. You are responsible for keeping it safe.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a list with "Delete list", or remove a single pilgrim, inside the extension.
  • "Forgot it? Erase all lists" on the unlock screen erases everything the extension has saved.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome also deletes everything it has saved.

If you forget your passphrase, your lists cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

No affiliation

Pilgrim Autofill is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD).

Changes to this policy

If the extension changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
